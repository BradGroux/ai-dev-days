## What changes for learners and facilitators

Canonical labs now exclude sensitive values from provider-readable notes,
verify the selected input/workspace, and permit a successful review without
inventing a defect. A timed plain-text offline exercise supplies a real output
rubric while recording that no model ran. Help, accommodation and declining to
share do not require prior contribution or participation debt.

The vendor demo distinguishes submitted evidence from format checks and
independent verification. Beaver Badges and the Personal AI demo recover from
unavailable browser storage; malformed saved state is narrowed to supported
fields. Browser-only persistence remains local and fallible; export supports
continuation. Vite 8.2.2, React plugin 6.1.1 and React DOM types 19.2.5 integrate
the pending development dependency updates without changing runtime versions.

Research source dispositions replace full transcript republication where no
reuse permission was recorded and explicitly supersede unsafe setup advice.
The earlier commits remain historical; this correction does not establish rights
for previously distributed copies.

## Edition and adoption consequences

Calendar numbering applies prospectively to program content. Further releases
on the same UTC date append .1, .2, etc. Historical tags, citations, reviews,
decisions and date-first event folders retain their original identities. No
dated aliases are created. The date is not a compatibility guarantee.

Commons adoption is independent and has no local deviations. Its human-boundary
clarifications and bounded conflict handling are substantive: local-file storage
is not permission to disclose to a provider; contribution creates no right to
participation or publication; a passing review need not produce a failure.
Review these choices when reusing older material. Commons tooling and methods
remain local to Commons. AI-Native teaching remains optional at its separate pin.

## Verification and limits

The release process requires clean installation, metadata regressions, redacted
publication/history scans, local/external link checks, existing and new vendor
adverse tests, deck/PDF parity, storage regressions, typecheck/build and browser
smoke. Independent content and standards review and the final merged-tree gate
are recorded in the [audit disposition](../reviews/edition-2026-09-05.md).
The public release is verified separately against its annotated tag and exact
committed body by `scripts/verify-published-release.mjs`.

No field effectiveness, comprehensive accessibility certification or specialist
approval is claimed. HOUBAs delivery/outcomes remain unverified; Aggie comparative
runs, recordings and redemption checks remain event-owner work. Existing event
PDFs/PNGs are preserved, with future-use corrections documented separately.
External rate limits and the local link parser's fragment/reference limitations
remain visible. The old local v1.0.0 tag differs from the remote after historical
republication; neither is overwritten. See the runbook for exact identities.

This release adds no separate binary assets. Existing teaching assets remain in
the repository and source archives. New publication never rewrites an older tag.
