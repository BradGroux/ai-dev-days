# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Participation in AI Dev Days should make the research and education clearer,
safer, more useful, and easier to trust.

## Expected Conduct

Participants must:

- discuss ideas, sources, and evidence without attacking people;
- represent experience, authority, review, assistance, and uncertainty
  honestly;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, consent, intellectual property, and
  professional boundaries;
- make room for correction, dissent, beginner questions, and different learning
  needs;
- keep criticism specific, relevant, and proportionate; and
- follow maintainer or facilitator instructions used to contain harm or restore
  a productive learning environment.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate
misrepresentation, doxing, publication of private or confidential material,
retaliation, sustained disruption, and knowingly unsafe guidance are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release activity, AI Dev Days events, and other spaces where someone represents
the program or its community.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the founding steward through a private contact
route listed on the
[`@BradGroux`](https://github.com/BradGroux) GitHub profile. Do not include
sensitive details in a public issue.

The steward or delegated facilitator may correct or remove content, limit
participation, pause an activity, close a discussion, or block a participant
when necessary. Decisions should state the reason and remain proportionate to
the conduct and risk. If the steward is materially involved, an uninvolved
reviewer should participate when practical and any governance limitation must
be recorded.
