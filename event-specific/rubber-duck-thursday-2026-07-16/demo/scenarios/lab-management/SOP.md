# SOP: Lab Sample Release Review

1. Match each sample to its method, batch, and chain-of-custody record.
2. Confirm required controls and duplicates are present.
3. Compare QC results with method acceptance limits.
4. Hold incomplete, failed, or conflicting records.
5. Draft reviewer questions and the evidence needed to clear each hold.
6. Present the packet to a qualified analyst and lab manager.

Stop on missing custody, failed QC, method mismatch, incomplete calculations, or unclear release authority. Do not alter results, sign a report, or release a sample.
