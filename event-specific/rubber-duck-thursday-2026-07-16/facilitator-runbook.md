# Facilitator Runbook

## Before Going Live

1. Open [slides.html](slides.html), [speaker-notes-30-minute.md](speaker-notes-30-minute.md), and [demo/index.html](demo/index.html).
2. Confirm the browser is in presentation mode and slide navigation works with arrow keys.
3. Confirm the demo scenario selector, Run Draft button, and Reset button work locally.
4. Keep [slides.pdf](slides.pdf) open as the first fallback.
5. Keep [demo-script.md](demo-script.md) open as the demo fallback.
6. Confirm the final slide QR code resolves to the SSTB.ai Discord invite: `https://discord.gg/Gmfkm7QVSF`.

## Live Sequence

1. Use slides 1-4 to frame the opportunity and the adoption problem.
2. Use slides 5-10 to explain the operating harness.
3. On slide 11, switch to the Accounts Payable scenario in the demo lab.
4. If time permits, show Field Operations or Supply Chain Logistics as the second scenario.
5. Return to slides 14-15 for the operating lesson and call to action.

## Demo Guardrails

- Say that every record is fictional before opening the demo.
- Keep all outputs labeled draft-only.
- Point to the human approval gate in every scenario.
- Do not paste private notes, credentials, contact data, or real business records.
- Do not connect live accounts or send anything externally.

## Handoff Line

"The model proposes. The workflow verifies. A person owns the decision."
