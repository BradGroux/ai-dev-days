# Rubber Duck Thursday: AI That Runs the Business

Date: July 16, 2026 at 1:00 PM Central / 2:00 PM Eastern
Format: GitHub Rubber Duck Thursday livestream
Hosts: Andrea Griffiths and Brad Groux
Audience: business leaders, operators, developers, and teams trying to move AI from demos into real work

This packet supports a practical OpenClaw Dev Day conversation about the AI opportunity outside software teams: invoices, scheduling, follow-ups, field work, lab operations, and supply-chain exceptions.

## Session Arc

1. Establish why AI success is still a people and workflow problem.
2. Show what large platform companies are signaling through implementation services.
3. Build the operating harness with a center of excellence, Markdown, SOPs, PRDs, and feedback loops.
4. Demonstrate five fictional, public-safe business-agent workflows.
5. Ask the audience to choose one painful workflow and ship one reviewable artifact.

## Demo Outcome

The demo lab contains five fictional scenarios:

- Accounts Payable exception review
- Marketing campaign brief assembly
- Field Operations daily dispatch handoff
- Lab Management sample-release review
- Supply Chain Logistics exception desk

Each scenario uses the same visible pattern: source notes, SOP, PRD, agent draft, deterministic checks, and human approval.

## Safety Boundary

Do not put secrets, API keys, tokens, credentials, private data, real customer records, live financial data, regulated lab data, shipment credentials, or non-public work material into the demo.

The examples are fictional. Agent outputs are draft-only. No email, payment, schedule, release, inventory, or logistics action is sent or executed from the demo.
