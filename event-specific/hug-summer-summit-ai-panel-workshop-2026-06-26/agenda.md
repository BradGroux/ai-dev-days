# Agenda - H.U.G. Summer Summit 2026 AI Panel and Workshop

- **Event:** H.U.G. Summer Summit 2026 AI Panel Discussion and Workshop
- **Date/time:** Friday, June 26, 2026, 1:00 PM-3:00 PM Eastern / 12:00 PM-2:00 PM Central
- **Format:** Virtual panel and interactive workshop
- **Co-presenters:** Arnold Castro and Brad Groux
- **Arnold Castro:** Professor of Practice, Texas A&M University Mays Business School
- **Brad Groux:** CEO, Digital Meld; OpenClaw contributor and maintainer

## Goal

Move attendees from "AI is interesting" to "I know one safe, practical way to
use AI agents with context, boundaries, and review."

## 1:00-1:05 Eastern - Welcome And Summit Frame

**Lead:** H.U.G.

- Welcome the H.U.G. audience.
- Set the education, workforce readiness, and community-impact frame.
- Remind attendees that hands-on work should use public or sanitized examples.

## 1:05-1:17 Eastern - AI To Agentic AI

**Lead:** Brad

Key points:

- AI uses data to make predictions or generate useful output.
- Generative AI changed the interface because people can work in language.
- Agentic AI adds goals, context, tools, memory, steps, and review.
- The durable skill is workflow literacy, not prompt tricks.

## 1:17-1:25 Eastern - OpenClaw And Agent Harnesses

**Lead:** Brad

Show:

- OpenClaw as a local-first control plane,
- memory as durable context,
- `AGENTS.md` as plain-text role and boundary instructions,
- channels as places the same bounded workflow can appear,
- loops and automations as repeatable work,
- the reading-support research agent as the safe first use case.

Emphasize that the process matters more than the model or harness.

## 1:25-1:55 Eastern - Panel Discussion

**Lead:** H.U.G. / Panel

Useful themes:

- future of AI in education and workforce readiness,
- career impact over the next 5-10 years,
- responsible and ethical use,
- human-centered AI and human judgment,
- entrepreneurship and community impact,
- preparing students for tomorrow's workforce.

## 1:55-2:00 Eastern - Workshop Handoff

**Lead:** Arnold / Brad

- Set up the hands-on block.
- Point attendees to [requirements.md](requirements.md).
- Use the fallback rule: if setup fails, observe or pair rather than losing the room.

## 2:00-3:00 Eastern - Interactive AI Workshop

**Lead:** Arnold

Recommended OpenClaw path if used:

1. Install OpenClaw.
2. Configure a model provider.
3. Open the dashboard.
4. Create memory and `AGENTS.md`.
5. Run a reading-support research prompt.
6. Review the output before use.
