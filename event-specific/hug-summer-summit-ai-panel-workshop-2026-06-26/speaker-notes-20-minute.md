# Speaker Notes - 20-Minute Flexible Version

Use this as a flexible opener, not a word-for-word script. If the panel starts
fast, cut to the core line and use the rest as answer-bank material.

Co-presenter context: Arnold Castro is a Professor of Practice at Texas A&M
University Mays Business School and leads the interactive workshop block.

## Core Line

AI gets useful when people stop treating it like magic and start treating it
like a junior teammate inside a harness: context, instructions, tools, memory,
review, and human judgment.

The process is more important than the model or harness. If the source
material, boundary, review, and next decision are unclear, a stronger model
only produces faster uncertainty.

## 5-Minute Version

1. AI is software that learns patterns from data and generates predictions,
   text, images, code, or decisions.
2. Generative AI made AI conversational, but a chatbot is still mostly a
   single interaction.
3. Agentic AI adds goals, context, tools, memory, and multi-step work.
4. OpenClaw gives that work a local-first control plane: model provider,
   memory, instructions, tools, channels, sessions, and visible loops.
5. The safe first nonprofit use case is research and drafting with public or
   sanitized data, followed by human review.

## 12-15 Minute Version

### Opening

Arnold is leading the hands-on workshop after the panel, so the job here is to
give the room a practical mental model before they touch tools.

AI is not new, but the interface changed. For years, AI mostly lived inside
systems people did not touch directly: recommendations, routing, search,
fraud detection, document processing. Generative AI made it feel like a
conversation.

That is useful, but it also creates a trap: people start thinking the chat box
is the product. It is not. The product is the workflow around it.

### What Is AI?

Keep it plain:

- AI is software using data to make predictions or generate useful output.
- Generative AI creates new text, images, audio, code, or plans.
- It is powerful because it can work across messy human language.
- It is risky when people treat fluent output as verified truth.

### What Is Agentic AI?

An agent is not just a chatbot with a better prompt. An agent has a goal,
context, tools, memory, steps, and review. It can inspect files, write drafts,
create tasks, run checks, and report back.

The key question is not "Can AI answer this?" The better question is "Can I
give an agent enough context and boundaries to help with this workflow?"

### What Is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is a local-first control plane for agent work. The useful mental
model:

- model provider: the AI brain,
- gateway: the local switchboard,
- memory: durable context,
- `AGENTS.md`: instructions and boundaries,
- tools: files, shell, browser, APIs, connectors,
- sessions: visible work history,
- automations: repeated checks or follow-ups.

OpenClaw is not the hero. The workflow is the hero.

Channels are where people meet the workflow: Discord for community follow-up,
Teams for internal work, WhatsApp, Telegram, and Signal for mobile-first
coordination. The same safety rule applies everywhere: humans approve
external sends and sensitive work.

### The Harness

The harness is the part most people skip:

1. source material,
2. memory,
3. instructions,
4. prompt,
5. tool access,
6. review prompt,
7. approval point,
8. follow-up loop.

This is how you move from "I asked AI something" to "we have a repeatable way
to improve a process."

### H.U.G. Relevant Example

For a reading-support organization, do not start with private student data.
Start with a public or sanitized research-agent workflow:

- collect public reading-support notes,
- ask the agent to summarize them,
- ask for practical activity ideas,
- force it to separate evidence from recommendations,
- run a skeptical review,
- have an educator or program lead approve before use.

That is useful, safe, and teachable.

## Panel Answer Bank

### Future Of AI In Education

AI will not replace the need for teachers, mentors, or community support. It
will reduce the friction around preparation, summarization, tutoring support,
translation, accessibility, and administrative work. The win is giving people
more time for judgment, relationships, and instruction.

### Workforce Readiness

The job skill is not "use a chatbot." The job skill is AI workflow literacy:
how to give context, check sources, protect data, break work into steps, review
output, and decide what should stay human-owned.

### Responsible AI

Responsible AI starts with boundaries. What data is allowed? What tools are
allowed? What can the agent draft? What requires approval? What evidence must
be checked before use?

### Human-Centered AI

Human-centered AI means the agent helps people do better work. It does not
make sensitive decisions alone, hide evidence, or remove accountability.

### Entrepreneurship And Community Impact

Small organizations can benefit because they have lots of repeated knowledge
work and limited staff time. The best first projects are low-risk: research
summaries, volunteer guides, checklists, grant-draft support, lesson planning
drafts, and meeting follow-ups.
