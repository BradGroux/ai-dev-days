# Scenario Cards

Use these when the room needs a concrete workflow quickly.

For every scenario, pick the process first: safe source, memory, `AGENTS.md`,
draft output, skeptical review, human approval, then channel.

## Scenario 1 - Reading Research Brief

**Use when:** educators or nonprofit leaders want public research summarized.

**Safe inputs:**

- public articles,
- public program descriptions,
- sanitized notes,
- approved links.

**First output:**

- short brief,
- evidence table,
- practical ideas,
- risks and limits,
- human review checklist.

**Channel fit:** Discord for follow-up questions, Teams for reviewed internal
drafts.

**Avoid:**

- private student data,
- donor records,
- staff records,
- confidential notes.

## Scenario 2 - Volunteer Orientation Checklist

**Use when:** the organization needs repeatable volunteer guidance.

**Safe inputs:**

- public program overview,
- sanitized volunteer role notes,
- approved onboarding expectations.

**First output:**

- orientation checklist,
- do/don't list,
- escalation path,
- review questions for staff.

**Channel fit:** Teams for staff review, WhatsApp or Signal only after the
message is approved.

**Avoid:**

- personal volunteer records,
- student names,
- background-check details,
- internal HR notes.

## Scenario 3 - Tutoring Activity Draft

**Use when:** educators want activity ideas for human review.

**Safe inputs:**

- grade-level goals,
- public reading standards,
- sanitized examples,
- approved lesson constraints.

**First output:**

- activity draft,
- materials list,
- adaptation ideas,
- review checklist.

**Channel fit:** internal document first, channel share only after educator
review.

**Avoid:**

- private student performance data,
- disability or medical details,
- disciplinary records.

## Scenario 4 - Community Partner FAQ

**Use when:** H.U.G. or a partner needs a clear public-facing draft.

**Safe inputs:**

- public organization description,
- approved program details,
- common public questions.

**First output:**

- FAQ draft,
- review notes,
- unclear claims,
- missing approvals.

**Channel fit:** public website or Discord only after organizational approval.

**Avoid:**

- private donor conversations,
- unreleased announcements,
- internal strategy notes.
