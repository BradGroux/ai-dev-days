# Post-Event Review

Complete this review after the event while evidence and decisions are still
fresh. Keep it proportionate to the event and safe for a public repository.

## Event record

- Event: `<EVENT_NAME>`
- Date: `<EVENT_DATE>`
- Accountable owner: `<ACCOUNTABLE_OWNER>`
- Review owner: `<REVIEW_OWNER>`
- Approved packet or revision: `<COMMIT_OR_VERSION>`

## Intended outcome

State the approved purpose, scope, audience, and expected outcome.

## Outcomes and evidence

Record public-safe evidence showing what occurred and whether the expected
outcome was achieved. Distinguish counts or observations from interpretation.
Do not include attendee identities, private responses, credentials, or
sensitive operational details.

| Outcome or claim | Evidence or source | Status and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `<OUTCOME>` | `<EVIDENCE>` | `<STATUS_AND_LIMITS>` |

## Exceptions, incidents, and recovery

Record material deviations, stop conditions, fallback use, recovery actions,
and unresolved risks. Assign owners to remaining actions.

## Decisions

Record material decisions, their authority, effective scope, and any
superseded guidance.

## Lessons

Separate observed facts, participant feedback, synthesis, assumptions, and
approved lessons. A lesson becomes program guidance only after accountable
review.

| Finding | Evidence and limits | Proposed disposition | Decision owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `<FINDING>` | `<EVIDENCE_AND_LIMITS>` | accept, propose, defer, supersede, or reject | `<OWNER>` |

Link accepted research evidence or proposed reusable changes to the appropriate
research note, issue, decision record, or pull request.

## Handoff and maintenance

| Action | Owner | Due or trigger | Evidence of completion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `<ACTION>` | `<OWNER>` | `<DUE_OR_TRIGGER>` | `<EVIDENCE>` |

Identify any curriculum, template, setup, control, or source material that
requires review. Link the tracking issue or pull request when one is created.

## Learner continuation

Record whether the approved continuation path was available and which
public-safe evidence, if any, shows learners could continue after the event.
Do not infer long-term use from attendance or a completed in-room demo.

## Retention and publication safety

State what durable material should be retained, corrected, superseded, or
removed under the applicable access, privacy, rights, and retention controls.
Do not treat chat history or storage location alone as authoritative.
