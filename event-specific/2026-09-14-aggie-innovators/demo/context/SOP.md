# Standard Operating Procedure: review a customer experiment

Owner: Jordan. Source: venture-brief.md. Trigger: new experiment or changed evidence.

1. Read the brief and explicit updates. Identify source IDs, observations, assumptions, target, and decision owner.
2. Keep interview interest separate from reservations. Free reservations are not paid demand.
3. Apply reservation updates by unique ID. Preserve original state and history; repeated cancellations must not double count.
4. Count active reservations and compare with the agreed target of five. Never lower the target to report success.
5. If below target, flag the gap and propose recruit/revise/pause for Jordan. If at or above target, request Jordan's review; do not approve automatically.
6. Check every requirement and record evidence or an unresolved gap. Missing/conflicting evidence stays uncertain; Riley maintains source records, Jordan decides.
7. Save a handoff: sources, original/current counts, changes, target, unknowns, decision status, next action and owner.

All demo actions are local. No student contact, real booking, payment, publication, or live account access. A reviewable draft is not a validated business or approved pilot.

Retain lessons for the next experiment; Jordan accepts any changes to the procedure.
