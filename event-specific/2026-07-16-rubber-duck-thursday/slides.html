Start with the work surface

Build the harness before you ask for magic.

A serious agent task should carry five boring fields. They move human judgment earlier and make the output reviewable.

01

Mode

Prototype, standard, or production?

02

Context

Which workflow, source files, owners, and systems matter?

03

Boundary

Least privilege per task: what may it read, write, send, or reach?

04

Verification

Which command, check, evidence, or rubric owns correctness?

05

Handoff

What changed, what passed, and what risk remains?

Communication is infrastructure

A center of excellence is a communication network, not a committee.

Bring the people who know the workflow, the data, the risk, and the support burden into the same loop.

Listen→Decide→Document→Review→Improve
Executive sponsorSets the outcome, removes blockers, and accepts business risk.
Workflow ownersKnow how the work actually moves and where it breaks.
Domain expertsCarry the judgment calls that rarely make it into process diagrams.
Data, IT, securityDefine sources, access, controls, observability, and support.
Builders and championsPrototype, test, teach, collect feedback, and keep the standard alive.

Skills are operating procedures

If you repeat an instruction twice, turn it into an SOP.

Chat is a good place to work through an idea. A repo is a better place for the standard.

Purpose and triggerWhy this workflow exists and what starts it.
PrerequisitesInputs, access, owners, and source-of-truth records.
StepsThe repeatable path, written at the level a new operator can follow.
Stop conditionsWhat the agent must never guess, send, approve, or change.
VerificationThe evidence and deterministic checks required before handoff.
HandoffWho reviews, who approves, and what is preserved for the next run.

Make the decisions durable

A PRD forces the questions the demo skips.

$grill-with-docs interviews the plan until the user, owner, source of truth, boundary, proof, and support model are explicit.

Messy notesEmails, interviews, spreadsheets, exceptions, and unofficial workarounds.
GrillChallenge assumptions, expose missing decisions, and identify risk.
PRDUsers, scope, inputs, outputs, boundaries, and open questions.
Acceptance checksDefine evidence, verification, stop conditions, and approval.
Build loopImplement the smallest safe slice and preserve the trace.
Who owns the decision?What is authoritative?What may the agent never do?What proves it worked?Who supports it later?
Reference: Public $grill-with-docs prompt
Bound the agent

The model proposes. Deterministic tools judge.

Context

Read the real workflow, artifacts, owners, and constraints.

→

One bounded action

Draft, classify, patch, or propose the smallest useful move.

→

Hard verifier

Run calculations, schema checks, tests, policy rules, or source reconciliation.

Invalid→Correct the actionBlocked→Ask for evidenceVerified→Human review
Stop condition: stop after repeated no-gain attempts, missing authority, or missing evidence. Report the best verified result.
Source: Brad Groux, “The Useful Part of Agentic Auto-Scheduling is the Feedback Loop”
Real-world use case 1
Accounts Payable

The agent assembles the three-way-match evidence and exception queue. A finance reviewer still owns approval and payment.

Fictional data · Draft only · No external actions
InvoiceRead vendor, PO, quantity, amount, and date.
ReconcileCompare PO, receipt, prior queue, and policy.
ClassifyReady for review, hold, or needs evidence.
ApproveAuthorized AP reviewer decides outside the agent.
Demo proof: one clean match, one quantity mismatch, and one likely duplicate. The agent drafts questions and sends nothing.
Real-world use case 2
Marketing

The agent turns approved facts and reusable assets into a campaign packet with a visible claim ledger.

BriefAudience, event facts, source assets, and constraints.

Real-world use case 3

Operational agents make constraints visible before work moves.

Field Operations

Draft the morning dispatch handoff without assigning crews or bypassing safety.

ReadWork orders, crew skills, equipment, access windows, permits.
FlagPermit hold, expiring inspection, late parts, unavailable truck.
ApproveDispatcher and safety owner make the assignment.

Lab Management

Assemble sample-release evidence without changing or releasing a result.

ReadSample, method, controls, duplicates, calculations, custody.
HoldQC failure, method mismatch, incomplete result, custody gap.
ApproveQualified analyst and lab manager own release.
Real-world use case 4

A supply-chain agent should run an exception desk, not the company.

Rank risk, show the evidence, and give the owner a bounded next decision. Keep clean shipments quiet.

ShipmentPriorityEvidenceHuman decision
SH-5204CriticalTemperature exceeded 25 C for 18 minutes.Quality owns product disposition.
SH-5207HighJob-critical material is projected 8.5 hours late.Operations confirms impact; logistics evaluates approved options.
SH-5201ClosedDelivered within plan at 10:42 AM.No action. Suppress from the active queue.
The pattern is the product: source packet → SOP → PRD → agent draft → verifier → human approval → feedback.
