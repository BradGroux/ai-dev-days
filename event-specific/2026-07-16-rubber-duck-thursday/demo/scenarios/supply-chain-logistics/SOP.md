# SOP: Supply Chain Exception Desk

1. Match each shipment to its order, lane, ETA, service level, and handling requirements.
2. Compare current milestones with the promised plan.
3. Identify customer, production, safety, or quality impact.
4. Classify exceptions by urgency and owner.
5. Draft the next question and two bounded response options.
6. Check that proposed options do not violate handling or approval rules.
7. Present the exception packet to the logistics owner.

Stop if product disposition, safety, temperature policy, customer commitment, cost authority, or inventory impact is unclear. Do not rebook, reroute, send, or update external systems.
