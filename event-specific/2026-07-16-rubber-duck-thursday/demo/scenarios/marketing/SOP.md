# SOP: Draft a Source-Backed Campaign Packet

1. Read the approved facts and asset inventory.
2. Identify the audience problem in plain language.
3. Draft one email, one social post, and one landing-page outline.
4. Attach a source note to every factual or performance claim.
5. Mark unsupported claims for removal.
6. Check dates, links, speaker names, and call-to-action consistency.
7. Present the packet for marketing and legal review.

Stop if a claim, date, audience list, consent basis, or approval owner is missing. Do not publish, schedule, email, or change a campaign system.
