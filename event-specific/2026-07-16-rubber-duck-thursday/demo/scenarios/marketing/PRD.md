# PRD: Marketing Campaign Brief Agent

## Problem

Campaign inputs are scattered across notes, asset folders, approved claims, and stakeholder feedback.

## Goal

Assemble a source-backed draft campaign packet that a marketing owner can review without chasing the inputs.

## Output

- campaign brief
- email draft under 180 words
- social post draft
- landing-page outline
- claim and source ledger
- open decisions

## Boundaries

- No CRM export
- No personal contact data
- No invented proof
- No publishing or scheduling
- No external sends

## Acceptance Criteria

- Dates and names match the approved brief.
- Every claim is supported or marked for removal.
- The call to action is consistent across assets.
- The packet lists unresolved approvals.
- The output is marked draft-only.
