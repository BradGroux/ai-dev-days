# Fabricated Company Memos

Status: fictional workshop source material.

## Memo 01: Risk Categories

Risk is an exposure that may become harmful.
Issue is a known problem already affecting recovery or operations.
Threat is an observed or reported adversarial pressure that may require escalation.

## Memo 02: Executive Brief Standard

Every executive brief item must include business impact, affected division, owner, evidence status, next action, and decision required.

## Memo 03: Local Prototype Boundary

The command center prototype is local-only.
It must not connect to live systems, create live records, send messages, or perform automated response.

## Memo 04: Evidence Confidence

Evidence states are reviewed, unreviewed, disputed, external request, and pending confirmation.
Low-confidence evidence may inform triage but may not justify a final recommendation.

## Memo 05: Communications Policy

Employee, customer, regulator, and press messaging must be drafted separately and reviewed before use.
The command center may identify communication needs but cannot publish language.

## Memo 06: Recovery Ownership

Every escalation must have an action owner and a decision owner.
The same person may hold both roles, but the fields must remain distinct.

## Memo 07: Simulated Repo Policy

The fabricated repo page exists to teach issue breakdown.
It must be clearly labeled as fictional and must not call GitHub.

## Memo 08: Cyber Content Safety

Workshop materials may describe risk categories, detection ambiguity, and business impact.
They must not include exploit instructions, payloads, credential examples, or steps for causing harm.
