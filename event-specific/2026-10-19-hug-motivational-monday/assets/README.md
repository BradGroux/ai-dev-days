# Visual assets

Line icons are unmodified Lucide SVGs from
https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/main/icons, downloaded September
14, 2026. Their ISC license is retained in `icons/LICENSE`. They are inlined
by the deck builder and inherit the existing theme colors. No runtime
dependency or external asset request is required.

`laundry-illustration.png` is a generated editorial illustration for the
explicitly fictional commercial laundry example. It is not evidence of a
real customer or engagement. Created with the built-in image generation tool.

Prompt: A route driver with a clipboard beside two rolling carts of folded
white hotel towels, with an industrial washing machine in the background.
Refined editorial illustration, landscape 3:2, restrained flat shapes with
light texture. Deep navy, muted slate, off-white, small teal and pink accents.
Focus on human counting and reconciliation. No text, logos, watermark,
panels, glow, or gradients. Readable at 500px wide in a dark presentation.
