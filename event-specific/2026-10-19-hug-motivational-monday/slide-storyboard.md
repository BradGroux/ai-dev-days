## Deliberate additions

- Building in public includes a privacy and ownership boundary.
- Sharing failures is treated as useful public work when it includes the lesson
  and protects the people involved.
- AI accelerates learning, but domain experts still validate consequential work.
- Consistency and usefulness matter more than chasing a viral post.
- The close gives the audience one small action instead of a broad aspiration.

## Story status

- Personal proof supplied: building in public led to Brad joining the OpenClaw
  team, hosting an event and recording a video with GitHub, and taking part in
  about a dozen speaking engagements and appearances.
- Customer example: a clearly fictional commercial laundry asks for an AI
  shortage prediction system. Observation reveals that rushed pickup counts
  and unowned exceptions cause the information gap. A one-route pilot tests a
  simpler capture and reconciliation process before any larger system.
