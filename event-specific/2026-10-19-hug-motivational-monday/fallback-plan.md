# Fallback Plan

## If screen sharing fails

Deliver from the speaker notes without slides. The talk does not depend on a
live demo. H.U.G. may show the approved PDF or screenshots from its own machine.

## If the session starts late

Use slides 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, and 11. Keep the one-week challenge and the final
line.

## If the connection becomes unstable

Turn off presenter video and keep audio. If Brad disconnects, the organizer can
pause the recording and use the provided contact channel to resume or
reschedule.

## If a personal or client question crosses a boundary

Decline specifics and return to the public or sanitized pattern. Do not put
secrets, API keys, tokens, credentials, client details, private data, or
sensitive personal information in the session, recording, shared files, or
public repositories.
