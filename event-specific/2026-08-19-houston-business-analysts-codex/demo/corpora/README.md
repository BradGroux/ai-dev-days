# Business Analyst Practice Corpora

These compact, fictional corpora let business analysts practice the same
source-to-decision method used in the Northstar vendor-onboarding demo without
repeating the procurement scenario.

| Corpus | BA practice | Prepared cases | Suggested time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Change-request impact analysis](change-request-impact-analysis/README.md) | scope, dependency, policy, data, communication, and release impact | ready, clarify, and stop | 25–35 minutes |
| [Stakeholder intake prioritization](stakeholder-intake-prioritization/README.md) | evidence quality, scoring, capacity, conflicts, and decision rights | ready, clarify, and escalate | 25–35 minutes |

Each corpus includes:

- raw stakeholder notes as discovery evidence;
- approved policy excerpts that take precedence over the notes;
- three structured fictional records;
- a run-ready analysis prompt; and
- a facilitator key with expected findings and acceptance checks.

## How to use one

1. Copy one corpus into a separate working folder.
2. Read its `README.md` and `prompt.md`.
3. Run the prompt against `source/` without opening `facilitator-key.md`.
4. Compare the result with the facilitator key.
5. Record disagreements as questions for the named human owner. Do not rewrite
   the source evidence to make the result fit the key.

## Framework boundary

The
[AI-Native Operating Framework](https://github.com/BradGroux/ai-native-operating-framework)
is an optional diagnostic lens for Intent, Responsibility, Work, Control,
Assurance, and Learning. It does not provide policy, approve an analysis, or
replace the accountable owners named in either fictional corpus.

## Public-safety boundary

Every company, person, process, policy, and record in these corpora is
fictional. Do not replace the fixtures with confidential or personal data in a
public repository, shared workspace, screenshot, or projected exercise.
