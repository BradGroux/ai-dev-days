# Stakeholder Notes: Improvement-Request Intake

- Organization: Cedar Grove Community Services (fictional)
- Captured by: portfolio business analysis team
- Status: raw discovery notes; not approved policy

## Portfolio manager

- Teams submit requests through a shared form, but benefit evidence is uneven.
- Requesters often mark everything urgent near quarter end.
- The delivery team has eight effort points available next quarter.
- The Portfolio Council wants a comparable view, not an automatic ranking.

## Service operations lead

- Appointment reminders could reduce missed visits, but the current request
  does not include a baseline no-show rate or consent-owner review.
- Staff manually call some clients today.
- Spanish and screen-reader support need to be considered before changing
  client communications.

## Accessibility coordinator

- The public service locator has a confirmed keyboard-navigation defect.
- A remediation deadline has already been accepted by the organization.
- Accessibility work should not have to compete on revenue or executive
  sponsorship.

## Executive services sponsor

- The sponsor wants a refreshed executive dashboard and called it the top
  priority.
- The request proposes pausing the service-locator remediation to free capacity.
- The sponsor also sits on the Portfolio Council.

## Data steward

- Dashboard metric definitions differ across departments.
- Any client communication using appointment or contact data needs a named data
  owner, purpose, minimum fields, retention, and consent basis.
- Estimated benefits are useful, but they must identify their method and owner.

## Discovery gaps

- baseline and target for appointment no-shows
- consent, language, accessibility, and exception behavior for reminders
- approved definitions and owner for dashboard metrics
- how the sponsor's Portfolio Council conflict will be handled
- whether eight effort points are still available after mandatory work
