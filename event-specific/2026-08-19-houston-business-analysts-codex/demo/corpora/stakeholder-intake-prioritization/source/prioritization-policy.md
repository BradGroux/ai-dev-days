# Approved Intake and Prioritization Policy

- Organization: Cedar Grove Community Services (fictional)
- Status: approved excerpts for the practice corpus
- Owner: Portfolio Council

These excerpts take precedence over stakeholder notes, urgency labels,
sponsor requests, and submitted scores.

## R-01: eligibility and required evidence

A request must name its problem owner, affected users, current-state evidence,
target outcome, measurable indicator, key dependencies, accountable benefit
owner, and estimated delivery effort. Missing or unsupported required evidence
produces **CLARIFY** unless an escalation condition applies.

## R-02: comparable scoring

Eligible discretionary work is scored from 1 to 5 on four dimensions:

- user value: 35%
- risk reduction: 30%
- strategic alignment: 20%
- time criticality: 15%

Use the weighted average rounded to two decimal places. A submitted score is a
claim until its evidence and owner are identified. Delivery effort and capacity
are reported separately; they are not hidden inside the benefit score.

## R-03: mandatory work

Accepted legal, regulatory, safety, security, or accessibility remediation is
mandatory. It is labeled separately, receives capacity before discretionary
work, and is not deprioritized because it lacks revenue or executive
sponsorship.

## R-04: capacity

The delivery team has eight effort points available for the quarter before
mandatory work is reserved. The Portfolio Manager confirms remaining capacity.
Only the Portfolio Council may defer accepted mandatory work or exceed
available capacity, and the decision must be recorded with risk ownership.

## R-05: conflicts and decision rights

Request sponsors may provide evidence and answer questions but may not approve
their own request. A Portfolio Council member with a material sponsorship
conflict must disclose it and abstain. The Portfolio Council owns final
funding and sequence decisions.

## R-06: data, communication, and accessibility

Requests using client data or changing client communications require Data
Steward, Privacy, Communications, and Accessibility review as applicable.
Prioritization readiness does not authorize data access, outreach, development,
funding, or release.

## R-07: readiness results

- **READY FOR PRIORITIZATION**: required evidence and owners support a
  comparable council review. It is not selection or funding.
- **CLARIFY**: required evidence, an owner, a dependency, or a supported score
  is missing or inconsistent.
- **ESCALATE**: the request asks to override mandatory work, capacity, a
  conflict rule, or another Portfolio Council decision boundary.
