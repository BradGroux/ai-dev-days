# Prompt: Analyze Stakeholder Intake and Prioritization

```text
Goal:
Produce a source-cited prioritization packet for [REQUEST_ID] and classify its
readiness as READY FOR PRIORITIZATION, CLARIFY, or ESCALATE.

Sources and authority:
Read README.md, source/prioritization-policy.md, the selected record in
source/intake-requests.json, and source/stakeholder-notes.md. Approved policy
takes precedence over submitted scores, sponsor requests, urgency labels, and
discovery notes. Preserve conflicts and unknowns instead of resolving them by
inference.

Framework lens:
Use the AI-Native Operating Framework concerns—Intent, Responsibility, Work,
Control, Assurance, and Learning—only to test whether the packet contains the
business context needed for a responsible decision. It is not Cedar Grove
policy and does not determine priority.

Produce:
- required-evidence completeness and eligibility;
- confirmed facts, unsupported claims, assumptions, conflicts, and questions;
- the weighted R-02 score, showing the calculation only when every component
  has identified support;
- effort, mandatory-work, capacity, dependency, data, communication,
  accessibility, and conflict impacts;
- accountable owners and the decisions they retain;
- one normal, one exception, and one prohibited acceptance scenario;
- exactly one readiness result with policy citations; and
- the smallest responsible next step.

Constraints:
Do not invent evidence, normalize unsupported scores, approve funding, reorder
the portfolio, displace mandatory work, contact stakeholders, or access a
business system. Use only fictional source data.

Done when:
The Portfolio Council can see what is supported, what is missing, how any score
was calculated, what capacity and policy boundaries apply, who decides next,
and why the readiness result follows the approved policy.
```
