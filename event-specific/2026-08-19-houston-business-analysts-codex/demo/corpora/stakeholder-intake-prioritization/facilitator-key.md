## RQ-201 score trace

`(4 × 0.35) + (4 × 0.30) + (3 × 0.20) + (3 × 0.15) = 3.65`

Keep the three effort points separate from the benefit score. Before selection,
the named owners still need to confirm checklist definitions and offline-mode
behavior.

### RQ-201 acceptance starters

- Normal: each dimension displays its submitted value, named evidence owner,
  source, weighted contribution, and the 3.65 total while effort stays separate.
- Exception: offline-mode confirmation fails; the dependency remains visible
  and the council decides whether to clarify, split, or defer the request.
- Prohibited: the analysis treats 3.65 as automatic selection or hides the
  three effort points inside the benefit score.

## RQ-202 clarification starters

- What is the approved no-show baseline, sampling period, and target?
- Who owns the measure and resulting benefit?
- What consent rule and minimum data are required for each channel?
- What happens for opt-outs, unavailable contact details, language needs, and
  assistive-technology users?
- Which evidence supports each proposed score?

Do not calculate a comparable score from sponsor estimates alone.

### RQ-202 acceptance starters

- Normal: after owners approve a baseline, target, consent basis, accessible
  templates, and score evidence, the request can return for comparable review.
- Exception: a client opts out or lacks an approved contact channel; the future
  workflow preserves service without sending a reminder.
- Prohibited: the packet calculates a comparable score from the sponsor's
  unsupported estimates or authorizes outreach before required reviews.

## RQ-203 escalation evidence

- Cite R-03 before reserving capacity for discretionary work.
- Cite R-04 for any request to defer mandatory work or exceed capacity.
- Cite R-05 for sponsor disclosure and abstention.
- Preserve the dashboard's metric-definition problem as a legitimate intake;
  escalate the requested override and conflict, not the need itself.

### RQ-203 acceptance starters

- Normal: mandatory work is reserved first, metric owners reconcile the
  definitions, and the sponsor abstains from the council decision.
- Exception: insufficient capacity remains after mandatory work; the Portfolio
  Council records whether to defer, split, or reject discretionary scope.
- Prohibited: the packet pauses accepted accessibility remediation or allows
  the sponsor to approve their own request.

## Portfolio-level observation

work is reserved. The Portfolio Manager must confirm remaining capacity after
the accessibility remediation is estimated. The analyst must not infer that
