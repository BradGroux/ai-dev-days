# Glossary

| Term | Working definition | Owner or source |
|---|---|---|
| Assistant | Draft-only review capability described in the PRD; never an approver or system actor | P-01 |
| Business owner | Employee accountable for the business need and initial request data | Process brief |
| CLARIFY | Required information is missing, unknown, or internally inconsistent | P-02, P-05 |
| Domain review | Human decision owned by Finance, Security, Legal, or Compliance | Process brief |
| PASS | Packet is complete enough for named human review lanes; not approval | P-05 |
| ProcureFlow | Fictional system of record used only after human-approved vendor creation | Stakeholder notes |
| Review lane | Named human function that must evaluate part of the packet | Process brief |
| Review packet | Draft collection of source facts, gaps, triggered lanes, and evidence | PRD |
| STOP / ESCALATE | A policy boundary prevents continuation until the named owner decides | P-04, P-05 |
| Unknown | Required value has not been confirmed; it is not equivalent to `no` | Data contract |

## Terms that remain unresolved

| Term | Why it is unresolved | Required owner |
|---|---|---|
| Low risk | Interviewees used the term without one approved definition | Procurement Operations and domain owners |
| Rush escalation | The request exists, but the escalation owner and permitted effect are not documented | Procurement Operations |
| Legal-spend threshold | Interviewees supplied conflicting values and no approved policy excerpt resolves them | Legal and Finance |

Do not use unresolved terms as decision rules.
