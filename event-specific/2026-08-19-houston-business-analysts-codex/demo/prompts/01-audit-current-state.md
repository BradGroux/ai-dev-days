# Prompt 01: Audit the Current State

```text
Goal:
Turn the raw vendor-onboarding notes into a source-grounded current-state audit
without inventing missing business rules.

Context:
Read AGENTS.md, framework-guidance.md, and source/stakeholder-notes.md only.
Do not read policy-excerpts.md or any completed fallback workspace during this
stage. Use the framework's six concerns as questions for completeness, not as
Northstar policy and not as six required headings.

Framework stage:
This is Understand. Do not skip to Approve or Use.

Constraints:
- Separate confirmed observations, assumptions, conflicts, and open questions.
- Identify triggers, actors, decision rights, inputs, outputs, systems,
  handoffs, exceptions, evidence, and learning gaps.
- Cite the stakeholder-notes section supporting each confirmed observation.
- Do not define low risk, invent a spend threshold, name an escalation owner,
  or turn audience ideas into approved rules.
- Do not take external action or use private data.

Done when:
Create drafts/current-state-audit.md with:
1. a concise current-state outline,
2. a six-concern coverage table,
3. the seven highest-value elicitation questions ranked by execution risk,
4. explicit unresolved authority and evidence gaps, and
5. a statement that the output is discovery synthesis, not approved policy.

After writing the file, summarize the three gaps most likely to cause unsafe or
inconsistent execution.
```
