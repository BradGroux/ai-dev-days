# Prompt 02: Draft the Process Brief

```text
Goal:
Turn the current-state audit and recorded audience input into a reviewable
process brief while keeping unresolved decisions visible.

Context:
Read AGENTS.md, framework-guidance.md, drafts/current-state-audit.md,
drafts/audience-decisions.md, source/stakeholder-notes.md, and
source/policy-excerpts.md. Policy excerpts govern wherever they apply. Audience
input remains discovery evidence until the named owner approves it.

Framework stage:
This is Document. The draft still requires Validate and human Approve before
Use.

Constraints:
- Preserve source precedence and cite P-01 through P-06 where applicable.
- Cover the framework concerns through clear business meaning, not forced
  headings.
- Name the process owner, performers, reviewers, decision owners, handoffs, and
  escalation gaps.
- Keep facts, proposals, conflicts, and open questions distinguishable.
- Do not silently resolve the low-risk definition, spend threshold, rush owner,
  questionnaire version, duplicate tolerance, or service levels.
- Draft only. Do not take external action.

Done when:
Create drafts/process-brief.md with status, scope, sources, current-state flow,
actors and authority, inputs and outputs, decision points, exceptions, stop
conditions, evidence, unresolved questions, next owners, and review limits.

Then report which unresolved decision most limits safe reuse and why.
```
