# Prompt 07: Adapt an Audience Workflow

```text
Goal:
Prepare an AI-readiness canvas for this generic workflow: [WORKFLOW].

Known context:
[PASTE ONLY PUBLIC-SAFE OR FICTIONAL DETAILS.]

Framework lens:
Use the AI-Native Operating Framework concerns—Intent, Responsibility, Work,
Control, Assurance, and Learning—to find missing business meaning. Do not claim
framework conformance and do not replace the organization's policy, lifecycle,
or accountable owners.

Framework stage:
Start with Understand, then propose the smallest useful Document and Validate
steps. Leave Approve, Use, and Improve with named accountable owners.

Produce:
- confirmed facts, assumptions, conflicts, and open questions,
- the six-concern readiness view,
- a people, process, data, guardrails, and proof canvas,
- the smallest useful SOP and PRD outline,
- one pass, one clarify, and one stop scenario,
- the three decisions a human owner must make before implementation, and
- the evidence and learning loop needed after a pilot.

Constraints:
Do not infer missing business rules, recommend connecting external systems, or
use private attendee details.

Done when:
The proposed first step is bounded, reviewable, low consequence, and clear
about ownership, approval, stop conditions, evidence, and what remains unknown.
```
