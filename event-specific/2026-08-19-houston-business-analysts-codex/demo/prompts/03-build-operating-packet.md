# Prompt 03: Build the Operating Packet

```text
Goal:
Build a small, internally consistent operating packet for the fictional vendor
review from the reviewed process brief and approved policy excerpts.

Context:
Read AGENTS.md, framework-guidance.md, source/policy-excerpts.md,
source/sample-vendors.json, drafts/current-state-audit.md, and
drafts/process-brief.md. Apply the AI-Native Operating Framework as the guiding
business lens. Apply Northstar policy as the decision authority.

Framework stage:
This is Document. Produce material for Validate; do not claim Approve or Use.

Produce under drafts/operating-packet/:
- README.md artifact map and source precedence,
- data-contract.md and glossary.md,
- AGENTS.md standing workspace rules,
- vendor-onboarding-sop.md,
- vendor-onboarding-assistant-prd.md,
- .agents/skills/vendor-review/SKILL.md,
- acceptance-scenarios.md, and
- verification-checklist.md.

Constraints:
- The SOP must communicate all eight framework content areas without copying a
  universal template mechanically.
- The PRD must define users, outcome, scope, non-goals, authority, requirements,
  acceptance criteria, and evidence.
- The skill must have one focused responsibility and may only prepare a draft
  review packet.
- Memory, conversation history, and framework guidance are not policy.
- Preserve human approval and prohibit external actions.
- Use only fictional source data.

Done when:
Return a traceability table from approved source → artifact rule → acceptance
scenario → evidence. Flag every unresolved decision and every requirement with
no test. Do not claim approval or framework conformance.
```
