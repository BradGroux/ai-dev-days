## Session thesis

Business analysts already do much of the difficult work required to make AI useful: understanding the current state, exposing exceptions, aligning stakeholders, defining data, setting boundaries, and deciding what evidence proves success.

Codex is the workbench for the session, but the durable method is the lesson. The method is to turn business knowledge into small, reviewable Markdown artifacts that people and agents can use together.
