## Product Need

Create a command center that helps E Corp answer:

1. Which cyber risks need executive attention today?
2. Which issues are blocking recovery?
3. Which threat signals are credible enough to escalate?
4. What evidence supports each escalation?
5. Who owns the next action?
6. Which decisions are unresolved?
7. What can be safely communicated to leadership?

## Required App Behavior

- local-only
- interactive
- readable by executives and operators
- evidence-linked
- exportable
- clearly fictional
- safe for workshop use

## First Useful Win

Show one screen where Angela can filter critical escalations, assign an owner, mark evidence reviewed, and export a concise executive brief.
