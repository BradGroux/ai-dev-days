# Company Brief: E Corp Cyber Recovery Scenario

This is fictional workshop material inspired by E Corp from Mr. Robot.
It is not canon, not operational guidance, and not real incident data.

## Company

E Corp is a global conglomerate headquartered in New York City.
The demo uses these divisions:

- Bank of E
- E Coin
- E Corp Labs
- E Phone
- E Healthcare
- E Shipping
- E Corp Recovery Program
- Corporate Communications
- Legal and Risk
- Technology Operations

## Timeline

The scenario takes place after the 5/9 crisis, while E Corp is trying to restore confidence across banking, identity, payments, logistics, and consumer services.

## Business Problem

Leadership receives cyber risk, operational issue, and threat signals from too many channels:

- executive email threads
- legal memos
- risk office summaries
- technology operations updates
- recovery program chats
- external agency requests
- employee reports
- fraud and availability signals

The company needs a local command center that lets stakeholders triage escalations, link evidence, brief executives, and plan follow-up work without connecting live systems.

## Product Target

E Corp Cyber Escalation Command Center.

The first version is a static, local-only app that turns fictional source material into:

- escalation queue
- evidence feed
- executive brief
- fabricated repo issue board
- build trace

## Safety Boundary

The app must not connect to live accounts, send messages, create live GitHub issues, trigger alerts, call external services, or include operational cyber instructions.
