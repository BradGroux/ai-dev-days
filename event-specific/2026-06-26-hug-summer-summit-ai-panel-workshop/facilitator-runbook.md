## First Success Metric

By the end of the workshop block, a successful attendee has:

- OpenClaw installed or a clear follow-up path,
- model provider configured or identified,
- dashboard open or observed on the facilitator machine,
- one reading-support research prompt drafted,
- one review prompt ready before using any output,
- one channel boundary named before anything is shared.

## Helper Lanes

- Green: setup works, keep building the research agent.
- Yellow: small install, PATH, provider, or browser issue.
- Red: stop local setup and follow the presenter machine.

## Public Safety Checkpoint

> Do not put secrets, API keys, tokens, credentials, private data, sensitive
> notes, or anything confidential in a public repository, shared file,
> screenshot, or projected prompt. When in doubt, keep it local.
