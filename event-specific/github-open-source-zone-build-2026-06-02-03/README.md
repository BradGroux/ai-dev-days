## Floor Goal

Show that OpenClaw is not just a chat UI. It is a personal AI assistant and local-first control plane for agent work across context, tools, channels, device nodes, sessions, and visible workflows.

For business conversations, make the point practical:

- reusable context beats one-off prompting
- bounded access beats uncontrolled automation
- visible workflows beat black-box agent output
- human approval stays in the loop for risky actions
- Veritas Kanban shows how OpenClaw-style work can become tracked delivery, not just conversation

## Recommended Conversation Lanes

| Lane | Time | Use When | Outcome |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hook | 30 seconds | Someone is walking by | They understand the one-line value. |
| Overview | 2 minutes | Curious but time-boxed attendee | They can explain OpenClaw to a teammate. |
| Business demo | 5 minutes | Founder, operator, PM, technical leader | They see intake to execution planning. |
| Maintainer demo | 5 minutes | OSS maintainer, GitHub-heavy engineer | They see issue triage, review, and release-readiness value. |
| Deep dive | 10-15 minutes | Serious adopter or integrator | They leave with install path, safety model, and follow-up action. |

## Core Line

> OpenClaw is a personal AI assistant you run on your own devices. The Gateway is the control plane that lets agents use durable context, bounded tools, channels, nodes, and visible workflows.

## Business Angle

Most businesses do not need a toy chatbot. They need a way to turn messy work into reviewable operating loops:

1. capture the right context
2. route it to the right role
3. apply policy and boundaries
4. produce an artifact or action plan
5. verify before anything is sent, shipped, or automated

That is the OpenClaw story to tell on the floor.
