## Box 1 — What was announced (the 2-3 that move the map)

1. **Dots: always-on agents.** Each dot gets its own cloud computer and browser, connects to 4,000+ apps, and carries context across ChatGPT, Slack, Teams, and voice. The form factor is a persistent worker with ongoing responsibilities, not a chat session you open and close.
2. **Plugin extensions: ChatGPT opens its front door.** Sidebar homes, interactive panels, and file viewers put a product inside ChatGPT for 1.2B weekly users. The same morning, OpenAI published the `openai/mcp-extensions` repo: a vendor `openai/*` layer on top of the neutral MCP Apps standard. Custom GPTs retire December 11, 2026, which is the forcing function.
3. **The access story, not a new flagship.** GPT-6.1 Sol ships at roughly a fifth of Astra's token prices ($2/$0.10/$10 per million input/cached/output) for agent loops you can afford to leave running. The planned GPT-6.1 Astra was pulled the day before the keynote over safety. Codex now runs on your machine, your phone, or the cloud, with reusable team environments.
