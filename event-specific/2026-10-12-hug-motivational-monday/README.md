## Purpose

Show young professionals why useful public work, community service, empathy,
and confidence in a repeatable problem-solving process matter more as AI makes
information and production easier to access.

The intended outcome is one practical commitment: each attendee identifies one
small thing they can learn, make, explain, or contribute in public this week.

## Core message

AI puts extraordinary tools and knowledge within reach. The lasting advantage
comes from how you approach problems, how well you understand people, what you
share, and whether others can trust the work they have seen you do.

Build in public where your professional community already gathers. Brad's
public work created the opportunity to join the OpenClaw team, host an event
and record a video with GitHub, and take part in about a dozen speaking
engagements and appearances. Help your local community without keeping score.
When a new problem arrives, trust your ability to learn enough to take the next
responsible step, then verify the result with the people who know the work.

## Public-safety boundary

Build in public without publishing client details, employer information,
private data, credentials, or work you do not own. Use public, fictional, or
sanitized examples. Do not put secrets, API keys, tokens, credentials, or
sensitive personal information in projected prompts, screenshots, shared
files, or public repositories.
