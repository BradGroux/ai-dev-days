## Current use

The original ingest included instructions and commentary about weakening
authentication and passing credentials through conversation. Those are not
approved workshop setup instructions. Do not weaken authentication to finish a
lab or paste credentials into exercise notes. Follow the
[official security guidance](https://docs.openclaw.ai/gateway/security) and the
[local setup track](../START-HERE.md); take the offline route when
safe setup is unavailable.

Current source review on 2026-09-05 confirms that OpenClaw documents trust
boundaries and a security audit. That review does not validate this video's
hosting, pricing, model, skill, or credential-handling claims.
